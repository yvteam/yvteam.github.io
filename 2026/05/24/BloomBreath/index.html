Calm Bloom: Calm Breathing

Calm Bloom is an offline-first companion for emotional balance, breathing resets, and gentle inner growth.
It combines daily affirmations, guided breathing exercises, mood journaling, classic quote reading, and weekly reflection so you can slow down, settle your emotions, and build a steadier self-care rhythm.
The experience is optimized for breathing exercises, daily affirmations and quotes, stress relief, calmer anxious moments, mindfulness routines, self-care habits, helping stressful, anxious, distracted, or low-energy moments feel more manageable.

TOP FEATURES
• Daily quotes and affirmations for a softer, more grounded start
• Breathing exercises with multiple rhythms, plus quote-linked and pure breathing modes
• Fast mood journaling with daily check-ins and streak tracking
• Weekly reflection, calendar history, and growth summaries that turn feelings into visible progress
• Themes, ambient sound, and breathing light for an immersive calming space
• Classic quotes and thoughtful reading beyond surface-level motivational copy
• Personalized reminders, display settings, language options, and listening styles
• Offline-first storage so your core content, logs, and stats stay private and dependable

Built for people who want a gentle pocket companion for stress relief, emotional regulation, and sustainable daily care.
