## Daily Weight Tracker - Calorie Counter & Food Diary

Daily Weight is a fast, ad-free weight loss tracker and fitness journal app designed to help users better control weight loss, burn fat, body shaping, build muscle, and maintain their ideal body composition. Support quick logging of weight, nutrition, water intake, bowel movements, menstrual cycle, and workout diary with comprehensive statistics and continuous updates. We believe it will help you achieve your health goals and manage your body transformation journey.
Daily Weight requires no registration, zero ads, with all data stored locally to maximize user privacy protection.

【Weight Loss Tracker】
Record daily weight, body fat percentage, BMI data at different times, with continuous weight trend charts to analyze progress curves at each stage. Set your target weight, maintain daily logging, and track your weight loss journey through weekly or monthly progress charts. Consistent tracking will bring you ultimate satisfaction and achievement.

【Food Logger & Nutrition Tracker】
Log daily meal plans, estimate calories, and food photos for easy review and comparison analysis of your eating habits.

【Water Intake & Hydration Tracker】
Scientific beverage classification, set hydration goals, quick check-in logging, and analyze daily target achievement for optimal wellness.

【Period & Menstrual Cycle Tracker】
Specially designed for female users to track menstrual cycles, making it easy to analyze how periods affect weight fluctuations and body changes.

【Workout & Exercise Logger】
Exercise has the most direct impact on weight management. Separately track cardio workouts and strength training to help users focused on fat loss or muscle gain with personalized analysis.

【Bowel Movement Tracker】
Track daily bowel movement frequency - digestive health is also an important factor affecting weight management and overall wellness.

【Daily Journal & Progress Notes】
Record every day of persistence, every progress milestone, and every insight on your fitness journey. Continuous review and reflection lead to better results and sustainable healthy habits.

Daily Weight covers all scenarios that impact weight management, supporting quick data entry and comprehensive tracking. We believe it will help you walk more easily on your weight loss and fitness transformation path, making it easier to maintain long-term healthy lifestyle changes.
