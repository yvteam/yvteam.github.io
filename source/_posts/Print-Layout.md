## Print Layout: Home A4 Print


Print Layout is a practical layout tool for home printing, photo layout, ID photo sheets, and real-size paper output.

Many households mainly use A4 paper and 4x6 photo paper, but everyday printing needs are often more specific:

You may want to print A5-sized content on A4 paper;
place multiple 1-inch or 2-inch ID photos on one A4 sheet;
arrange several photos at exact sizes on the same page for easy cutting;
create labels, cards, stickers, small business card samples, or text notes;
or combine images, PDFs, and text on one sheet at real print dimensions.

Print Layout lets you choose a paper size directly on your phone, such as A4, A5, A6, or 4x6 photo paper, then add other common print sizes onto that sheet.

For example, you can print standard 1-inch or 2-inch photos on A4 paper, place two A5 layouts on one A4 page, or mix different sizes on the same sheet, such as photos, ID photos, labels, cards, PDF pages, and text notes.

Besides built-in common sizes, you can also create custom dimensions. Whether it is a small label measured in centimeters, a fixed-size card, or an image that needs millimeter-level size control, you can place it on the page, adjust the layout, and export it as a PDF or image for saving, sharing, or later printing.

Built around print layout, home printing, A4 printing, A5 layouts, 4x6 photo paper, ID photo sheets, photo layout, label printing, cards, stickers, PDF printing, paper size, and millimeter dimensions, Print Layout makes it easier to arrange multiple real-size items on one sheet.

KEY FEATURES

• Supports A4, A5, A6, A3, B5, Letter, Legal, and common photo paper sizes
• Designed for everyday home printing with A4 paper and 4x6 photo paper
• Place A5 content, 1-inch photos, 2-inch photos, labels, cards, and more on A4 paper
