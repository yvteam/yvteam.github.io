Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026

InSketch ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by MicroFish. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use InSketch.

1. Information We Collect

InSketch is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Photos: Photos you select or capture within the App are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos to any server.
  • Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (e.g., app launches, feature usage) to improve the App. This data cannot identify you personally.
  • Advertising: For non-VIP users, we use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization, subject to your consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's functionality
  • To process your subscription status
  • To display relevant advertisements (non-VIP users only)
  • To analyze app usage trends and improve user experience

3. Photo Processing

All image processing — including sketch effects, AI style transfer, and paper textures — is performed locally on your device. Your photos never leave your device and are not transmitted to any external server.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Analytics data collected by Firebase is retained according to Firebase's data retention policies. You can reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

InSketch is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Opt out of ad tracking via iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking
  • Reset your advertising identifier via iOS Settings > Privacy > Advertising
  • Delete the App and all locally stored data at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
