Adult Emoji - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 20, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Adult Emoji collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Emoji favorites (stored locally on your device)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Feedback and support communications (if you contact us)

Automatically Collected Information

  • Anonymous app usage statistics (crashes, performance data)
  • Device type and iOS version (for compatibility)
  • No personal identification information is collected

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email, or phone number (unless you contact us)
  • Location data
  • iMessage conversations or content
  • Contacts or photos
  • Any sensitive personal data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain App functionality
  • Improve user experience and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests
  • Analyze performance anonymously

3. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Favorites and settings are stored only on your device using iOS UserDefaults
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not automatically sync data to any cloud service
  • Export / Import: You fully control your data

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our legal rights and safety
  • With your explicit consent

Third-Party Services

  • No analytics SDKs
  • No advertising networks
  • No trackers

5. iMessage Extension Privacy

  • Operates entirely on your device
  • We cannot access or read your conversations
  • Messages are handled by Apple’s Messages app

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If such data is discovered, we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Security

  • Local storage using iOS secure mechanisms
  • No external data transmission (except crash reports, if enabled)
  • Secure distribution via the Apple App Store

8. Your Rights

  • Access your favorites and settings
  • Export data in JSON format
  • Delete data by uninstalling the App
  • Disable analytics (if implemented)

9. Data Retention

  • Local data remains until deleted by you
  • Crash reports are anonymized and retained for up to 90 days

10. In-App Purchase Data

  • We do not collect your payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple.
  • We may locally store your purchase status (e.g., unlocked features) to enable functionality.
  • No purchase data is shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced via in-app notifications or App Store update notes.

12. International Users

By using the App outside your country, you consent to data handling under this policy.

13. Contact Us
